Can you summarize the changes in capital requirements from the new CBK circular for Tier 1 banks?

Jonathan Vickers • 10:42 AM
smart_toy

The new CBK circular introduces several adjustments for Tier 1 institutions, specifically focusing on the transition from Basel II to Basel III liquidity frameworks. Key changes include:

1. Minimum Core Capital: Increased from KES 1B to KES 2.5B over a three-year glide path.
2. Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR): A new buffer of 2.5% on top of the existing 10.5% statutory requirement is mandatory.
3. Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA): Re-evaluation of sovereign debt holdings in line with recent credit rating shifts.
