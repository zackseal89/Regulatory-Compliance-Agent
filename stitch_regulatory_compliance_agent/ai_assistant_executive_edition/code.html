Regulatory Analysis

The CBK Circular 15/2026 establishes a revised framework for Data Protection & Privacy (DCP) specific to Digital Credit Providers. Analysis of the directive reveals three high-impact requirements:

01. Sovereignty of Data

Mandatory domestic infrastructure for all sensitive financial records. External hosting requires explicit Central Bank authorization.

02. Granular Consent Framework

Prohibition of aggregate consent models. Individual third-party sharing necessitates discrete opt-in mechanisms.

03. Incident Reporting Threshold

Strict 72-hour window for disclosure of data compromises to the Central Bank from the moment of detection.

Status: Critical Compliance Deadline: October 2026.
